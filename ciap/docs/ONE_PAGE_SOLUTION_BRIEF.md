# CIAP One-Page Solution Brief

CIAP is an AI-driven crime intelligence and analytics platform for Karnataka Police. It uses the official FIR ERD as the system of record and builds intelligence on top of `CaseMaster`, `Accused`, `Victim`, police, location, court, arrest, and chargesheet entities.

## What It Delivers

- Automatic FIR intelligence scoring.
- Knowledge graph link analysis.
- Crime hotspot replay and prediction overlays.
- Repeat offender and gang discovery.
- Explainable AI recommendations for patrol and investigation.
- SCRB executive reports with trends, district ranking, forecasts, and deployment guidance.

## Why It Matters

CIAP turns FIR data into preventive policing decisions. It helps leaders see where crime is rising, why risk is high, who is connected, and what action should be taken next.
